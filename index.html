About Us

Small by design, so everyone is known.

Our community sits in a quiet South Weber neighborhood at the mouth of Weber Canyon, minutes from Ogden Regional Medical Center and close to family across Davis and Weber counties.

We've cared for residents here since 2019 as Petersen Farms. Now as Hearthstone South Weber, we're building on that foundation: attentive caregivers, home-cooked meals, and a calendar full of things to look forward to.

Care Options

The right support, at the right level.

Every resident gets a personal care plan built with them and their family, and we revisit it as needs change.

Assisted Living

Private studio apartments for seniors who want independence with a helping hand.

  • Help with bathing, dressing, and transfers
  • Medication management
  • 24-hour call system and staff
  • Three meals a day, with special diets accommodated
  • Housekeeping and laundry

Memory Care

A secure, calm setting for residents living with Alzheimer's disease or other forms of dementia.

  • Private memory care apartments
  • Secured environment with 24/7 supervision
  • Structured daily routines and engagement
  • Incontinence and non-ambulatory care
  • Regular updates for families

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Hearthstone South Weber the same place as Petersen Farms?

Yes. Petersen Farms Assisted Living & Memory Care is now Hearthstone South Weber. We're at the same address, 6980 South 475 East in South Weber.

What's the difference between assisted living and memory care?

Assisted living is for seniors who are largely independent but need help with daily tasks like medications, bathing, or dressing. Memory care adds a secure setting, more supervision, and structured routines for people living with dementia.

How much does it cost?

Cost depends on the apartment and the level of care each resident needs. Call us and we'll walk you through current availability and pricing.

Can we visit before deciding?

Please do. We'd love to show you around, introduce you to our team, and answer your questions in person. Call to set up a time.
